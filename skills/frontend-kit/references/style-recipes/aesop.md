## School

Editorial / minimalist. Quiet luxury: the page earns attention by
restraint, not by decoration.

## Best for

Apothecary pages, quiet premium commerce, skincare / fragrance /
home-goods stores where the product and the photography are the
design.

## Visual language

- One warm neutral ground for the whole page; text in a single dark
  ink. Two colours doing all the work — contrast, not variety,
  carries the hierarchy.
- A humanist serif reserved for emotive headlines, set against a
  neutral grotesque sans for everything functional (nav, body,
  labels). The serif/sans tension IS the system's personality.
- Sharp corners, no shadow anywhere. Flat grounds, hairline rules.
- Full-bleed photography does the visual work; type stays quiet on
  top of it. One small uppercase tracked label, one headline, one
  outlined CTA per hero — repeated, not re-invented per section.
- Motion is restrained: opacity and short colour transitions. Nothing
  flies in.

## Signature moves

- Eyebrow + headline-over-photograph + outline-CTA hero pattern,
  repeated on every hero.
- A dark closing footer over the light page — the mode flip that
  ends the page.
- Long, unhurried measure; generous section air; nothing competes
  with the product photograph.

## Anti-patterns (do not do these in this direction)

- A third accent colour "for energy" — the two-colour discipline is
  the direction; a third colour breaks it.
- Gradients of any kind, decorative shadows, rounded "friendly"
  cards — all read as a different brand.
- Emoji as icons; drawn illustrations standing in for product
  photography; fabricated claims in the copy.
