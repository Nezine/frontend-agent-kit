## School

Editorial / minimalist, magazine register: the contents page as the
design — an index that is itself worth reading.

## Best for

Magazine contents, editorial indexes, issue / collection pages,
any page whose job is to list many pieces and make each one sound
worth opening.

## Visual language

- A strong masthead and a strict column grid; the grid is visible
  in the alignment even when no rules are drawn.
- Serif display for story titles, small caps / tracked labels for
  sections and page numbers, sans for standfirsts and metadata.
- One photograph per story, editorially cropped; the image and the
  title share one column so the eye reads them as a unit.
- Numbering is design: issue number, page numbers, section numbers
  are set, not hidden.

## Signature moves

- The contents list as typography: title, standfirst, page number —
  three lines, same recipe, repeated down the page.
- A single restrained accent reserved for section markers and the
  current-issue flag — used twice, never as decoration.
- Generous top air under the masthead; the first story starts low,
  like a magazine's first spread.

## Anti-patterns (do not do these in this direction)

- Cards with shadows and rounded corners — this is a magazine, not
  a dashboard.
- Emoji, stock icons, or "trending" badges on stories.
- A gradient hero or a full-bleed video header — the contents page
  is the hero.
