## School

Warm humanist. A book page: the reading experience is the product,
and the page should feel like good paper.

## Best for

Book detail pages, long-form reading pages, essays, documentation
that wants to be read rather than scanned.

## Visual language

- Warm paper ground, dark ink, one quiet accent. The measure is
  narrow (reading measure, not a marketing full-width) and the
  leading is generous.
- Serif for the reading text — the book's voice — with a calm sans
  for navigation, metadata, and the purchase action.
- The book object itself is the visual: cover, spine, or an open
  spread, photographed or rendered plainly. No illustration stands
  in for the book.
- Hierarchy is typographic: title, author, chapter, pull-quote.
  Nothing else competes.

## Signature moves

- A long excerpt set beautifully, with a pull-quote that earns its
  space — the excerpt sells the book, not the adjectives around it.
- Chapter / section markers as quiet numerals in the margin.
- The purchase action is one calm button, repeated at the top and
  the bottom, identical both times.

## Anti-patterns (do not do these in this direction)

- A gradient hero, a video autoplay header, or a "bold" display
  face — all fight the reading register.
- Cards, shadows, and badges around the book — the book is not a
  dashboard widget.
- Fabricated praise ("everyone is talking about it") — quote real
  praise or print none.
